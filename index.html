03 / How I work

From problem
to proof.

Good engineering is more than a polished model. It's a traceable process with clear constraints, justified decisions, and results you can explain.

STEP 01 / DEFINE

Understand the problem

Identify requirements, constraints, loads, interfaces, and what success needs to look like.

STEP 02 / DEVELOP

Model and evaluate

Explore concepts, create CAD or analytical models, and compare options against the requirements.

STEP 03 / VALIDATE

Test and improve

Check assumptions, interpret results, document trade-offs, and iterate based on evidence.
